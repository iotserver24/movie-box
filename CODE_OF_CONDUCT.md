# Code of conduct

Movie Box welcomes respectful participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different perspectives and assume good faith while addressing concrete behavior.
- Give credit for others' work and respect privacy and intellectual property.
- Keep discussions relevant and accept reasonable moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, disclosure of private information without consent, spam, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also prohibited.

## Reporting

For non-sensitive conduct problems, ask a maintainer to review the relevant public discussion. For sensitive concerns, request a private contact channel without posting identities, private messages, or other sensitive details publicly. No dedicated confidential conduct-reporting inbox is currently documented. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, give warnings, restrict participation, or ban participants based on severity and repetition. They should explain decisions where appropriate while protecting affected people's privacy. Concerns about a moderation decision may be raised with the maintainer respectfully and without exposing private information.
